# es-core (12 clips)

Short dictations for the Español + English pair, modeled on gate-core: two Spanish sentences
with names and terms in their own context, one of them with a forbidden word, two with numbers,
one plain sentence with four unrelated words as context that must not appear, the four English
sentences of gate-core unchanged, and three Spanish sentences with English words, two with their
terms as context and one plain. Each sentence runs with its own context.

Ximena is said like Jimena, so `say` has Jimena and the reference the vocabulary's spelling.
Itzel Yáñez and Basecamp test a name and a product the model may not know. The number sentences
list other spellings of each number in `accepted`.

The Spanish text of this set was written and reviewed by an AI process, not by a native speaker,
and waits for a native reader. Until one has read it, its references, its accepted spellings and
every result on it rest on AI review only.
