# es-homophones (36 clips)

Vocabulary words that sound like common Spanish words, for the Español + English pair, as
homophone-distractors does for Chinese and English: Cursor and cursor, Notion and noción, Ruby
and rubí, Python and pitón, Jira and gira (a tour), Miro and miro (I look), Claro and claro (of
course), and the given names Luz and luz (light), Sol and sol (the sun), Blanca and blanca
(white). Every clip runs with all 10 words as context, the way a user's vocabulary would hold
them. 18 clips say the common word and list the vocabulary word in `forbid_terms`. 18 say the
vocabulary word, some next to the common word, and list it in `expect_terms`. The reference is
the sentence.

A term with Latin letters matches with its case, so the plain words count as the vocabulary word
only when the model writes them with a capital, and every common word sits inside its sentence,
never first.

The Spanish text of this set was written and reviewed by an AI process, not by a native speaker,
and waits for a native reader. Until one has read it, its references, its accepted spellings and
every result on it rest on AI review only.
