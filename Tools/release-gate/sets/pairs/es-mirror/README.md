# es-mirror (60 clips)

Spanish sentences with English words for the Español + English pair, spoken by Paulina. The four
mirror sets hold the same 60 situations of `../mirror-template.jsonl`, each written the way a
speaker of the language who mixes in English would say it, not translated word for word, so
their rates compare:

- m01 to m20: office and developer terms (PR, deadline, deploy, Slack, Jira, sprint, API and
  more)
- m21 to m30: names of people and companies
- m31 to m40: numbers, dates, times and money
- m41 to m50: short replies
- m51 to m60: self corrections, which the reference keeps as spoken

Every clip runs without context, so the set measures the error rate and the recall of the
English words as the model writes them on its own.

The sentences are Latin American Spanish with tú and keep the English words in Latin letters, so
Paulina says them with Spanish sounds, as many speakers do. Numbers follow the Spanish of
Mexico: digits with a comma for thousands (1,200 dólares) and a colon for minutes (3:30), and
words for small counts (para tres). A period for thousands, as many other Spanish speaking
countries write it (1.200 dólares), counts as right too. Where a number may also be written in
words, the words are an accepted spelling, as are other spellings of a few words (okey for OK,
email for mail).

The Spanish text of this set was written and reviewed by an AI process, not by a native speaker,
and waits for a native reader. Until one has read it, its references, its accepted spellings and
every result on it rest on AI review only.
