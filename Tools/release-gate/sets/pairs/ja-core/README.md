# ja-core (12 clips)

Short dictations for the 日本語 + English pair, modeled on gate-core: two Japanese sentences with
names and terms in their own context, one of them with a forbidden word, two with numbers, one
plain sentence with four unrelated words as context that must not appear, the four English
sentences of gate-core unchanged, and three Japanese sentences with English words, two with
their terms as context and one plain. Each sentence runs with its own context.

In `say` the terms are written as a Japanese speaker says them (クバネティス, てしがわらさん), and the
reference has the vocabulary's spelling (Kubernetes, 勅使河原). The number sentences list other
spellings of every number but the room number 403 in `accepted`.

No native speaker has read the Japanese text of this set yet. Until one has, its references, its
accepted spellings and every result on it are not confirmed by a native reader.
