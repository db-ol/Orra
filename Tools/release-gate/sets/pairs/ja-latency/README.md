# ja-latency (13 clips)

The say clips for timing release to paste with the 日本語 + English pair, modeled on latency-say:
five Japanese dictations (two short, two of middle length and one long), the five English clips
of latency-say unchanged, and three Japanese dictations with English words, one short, one of
middle length and one long. The Japanese ones say the same things as the Chinese clips of
latency-say, without fillers, since Orra has no Japanese filler rule yet. The self correction in
ja_mid_1 stays in the reference, as in latency-say, and numbers are digits.

No native speaker has read the Japanese text of this set yet. Until one has, its references, its
accepted spellings and every result on it are not confirmed by a native reader.
