# ko-core (12 clips)

Short dictations for the 한국어 + English pair, modeled on gate-core: two Korean sentences with
names and terms in their own context, one of them with a forbidden word, two with numbers, one
plain sentence with four unrelated words as context that must not appear, the four English
sentences of gate-core unchanged, and three Korean sentences with English words, two with their
terms as context and one plain. Each sentence runs with its own context.

In `say` the terms are written as a Korean speaker says them (쿠버네티스, 그라파나), and the reference
has the vocabulary's spelling (Kubernetes, Grafana). 남궁민지, 제갈윤 and 두레이 test names and a product
the model may not know. The number sentences list other spellings of most numbers in `accepted`.
The building and the room number (2동, 403호) list none, since Korean writes them in digits only,
and 이 동 would read as this building.

No native speaker has read the Korean text of this set yet. Until one has, its references, its
accepted spellings and every result on it are not confirmed by a native reader.
