# ko-latency (13 clips)

The say clips for timing release to paste with the 한국어 + English pair, modeled on latency-say:
five Korean dictations (two short, two of middle length and one long), the five English clips of
latency-say unchanged, and three Korean dictations with English words, one short, one of middle
length and one long. The Korean ones say the same things as the Chinese clips of latency-say,
without fillers, since Orra has no Korean filler rule yet. The self correction in ko_mid_1 stays
in the reference, as in latency-say, and numbers are digits.

The Korean text of this set was written and reviewed by an AI process, not by a native speaker,
and waits for a native reader. Until one has read it, its references, its accepted spellings and
every result on it rest on AI review only.
