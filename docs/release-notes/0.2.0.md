Choose your own talk key, talk hands free, and get cleaner text.

- Choose the talk key by pressing it: fn (Globe), or the left or right Control, Option or Command key. A left key starts recording only after you hold it alone for a moment, so its shortcuts keep working.
- Press the talk key twice quickly to talk hands free. Press it once more to paste, or Escape to cancel.
- Fillers such as um, uh and 呃 are removed, and numbers spoken in Chinese, such as dates, times, prices and percentages, are written as digits. Settings turns each off.
- The vocabulary holds up to 5,000 words, in a table you can search and sort.
- Learning from corrections now catches more fixes, such as Ora to Orra and Aura to Orra. When you change one character of a Chinese name, Orra asks before adding it.
- With a vocabulary, Orra no longer pastes vocabulary words when nothing was said.
- A calmer indicator: white bars in a small dark capsule.
- Choose Orra's language in Settings, and read each setting's explanation right under its switch.
- Report a Problem… in the menu opens a GitHub issue with your Mac's details filled in.

自选说话键，可以免手持说话，文字也更干净。

- 按一下就能选好说话键：fn（地球仪）键，或者左侧、右侧的 Control、Option、Command 键。左侧的键要单独按住片刻才开始录音，所以它们的快捷键照常可用。
- 快速连按两下说话键，就能免手持说话。再按一下粘贴，按 Esc 键取消。
- 自动去掉“呃”“um”“uh”这类语气词，并把说出来的日期、时间、价格、百分比等数字写成阿拉伯数字。两项都可以在设置里关闭。
- 个人词库最多可以放 5,000 个词，以表格显示，可以搜索和排序。
- 从修改中学习能识别更多改动，比如把 Ora 改成 Orra、把 Aura 改成 Orra。改了中文名字里的一个字时，Orra 会先问你再添加。
- 有个人词库时，没说话也不会再粘贴出词库里的词。
- 指示条更安静：深色小胶囊里的一排白色音量条。
- 可以在设置里选择 Orra 的界面语言，每个开关的说明就在开关正下方。
- 菜单里的“报告问题…”会打开 GitHub，并填好你的 Mac 的信息。
