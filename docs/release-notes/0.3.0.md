Clean up your dictations and translate them with a local model, and find every setting on one General page.

- Clean Up Text, off by default, runs a local model (Qwen3.5 4B, a 3.05 GB download) after the filler and number rules. It removes the fillers the rules leave and applies spoken self corrections such as "Monday, I mean Tuesday", and keeps words that carry meaning. Paste Original Text in the menu brings back the text from before the cleanup.
- Translation: hold the translation key, right Option unless you pick another, and Orra pastes what you said in English or Chinese. It uses the same model on your Mac. Translate into picks automatic, English or Chinese.
- The cleanup model leaves memory after 15 minutes without a dictation and loads again when you press the key.
- Settings has a sidebar with General, Vocabulary and About. General holds the keys, the features, dictation, the microphone and the system settings, and About shows the version and the updates.
- Words you taught Orra by correcting them are now written your way even when the speech model mishears them again.
- A transcript that comes out in a third language, such as Swedish for Mandarin, is transcribed again in Chinese or English.

用本地模型整理和翻译你的听写，所有设置都在“通用”一页里。

- “整理文字”默认关闭，打开后会在语气词和数字规则之后运行一个本地模型（Qwen3.5 4B，下载 3.05 GB）。它会去掉规则漏掉的语气词，按你说出口的更正改好内容，比如“三点，哦不对，四点”，有意义的词会保留。菜单里的“粘贴原文”可以找回整理之前的文字。
- 翻译：按住翻译键（默认是右侧 Option 键，可以更改）说话，Orra 会把你说的话翻译成英文或中文再粘贴。翻译用的是同一个本地模型，在你的 Mac 上运行。“翻译成”可以选自动、英文或中文。
- 15 分钟没有听写时，整理模型会从内存中卸载，下次按键时重新加载。
- 设置改成侧边栏，有“通用”“个人词库”“关于”三页。“通用”里有按键、功能、听写、麦克风和系统设置，“关于”里显示版本和更新。
- 你改正过的词，语音模型再次听错时，Orra 也会写成你的写法。
- 如果转写结果变成了第三种语言，比如把普通话写成瑞典语，Orra 会用中文或英文再转写一次。
