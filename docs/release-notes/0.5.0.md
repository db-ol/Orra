Orra comes in Japanese and Spanish, with dictation and translation in them as a beta, and a dictation can now run up to 5 minutes.

- Orra's menus and windows are now in Japanese and Spanish too. Orra follows the Mac, or you pick a language under Interface language in Settings, General. The Spanish is Latin American Spanish. The README, user guide and privacy page come in both languages, and the update window shows these notes in the language of Orra's interface.
- Dictation language in Settings, General picks the language you speak besides English: 中文（普通话和粤语）+ English, 日本語 + English (Beta) or Español + English (Beta). You can mix the two languages in one sentence. A Japanese or Spanish sentence is pasted as the speech model wrote it. Before, Orra was slow with such speech and sometimes pasted it translated into Chinese or English. With Japanese or Spanish, Clean Up Text and Learn from my corrections work on English sentences only for now. Pick Japanese or Spanish only if you dictate in it.
- Translation goes between English and your dictation language, so the translation key turns Japanese or Spanish into English, and English into your dictation language. Translate into offers Automatic, English and your dictation language. Live Captions translate the same way, and Speak Translations to Others speaks Japanese and Spanish translations too.
- A dictation can run up to 5 minutes instead of 1, with a countdown in the last 15 seconds. Orra transcribes a recording over a minute in pieces cut at pauses, which takes about 2 seconds per minute of speech on an M5 Pro, and up to twice as long in Chinese with a vocabulary.
- Use names on screen (Beta) in Settings, General is new and off until you turn it on. When you start a dictation, Orra gives the speech model the names in the text before the cursor and in the window title, so a name on the screen comes out spelled the same way. The text itself never goes to the model and is never saved. Orra never reads password fields, an app with secure input on, or the password managers 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePassXC, Apple's Passwords and Keychain Access. A known risk: when a name on the screen sounds like a different name you say, Orra may write the one on the screen.
- Meeting Interpretation has its own page in Settings, right below General, with all the settings of Live Captions and Speak Translations to Others. Translation stays on General.
- A new user's talk key starts in Press to start and stop, and the welcome window offers Hold and Double press. A talk key you set up before keeps its mode.
- In Cantonese 呃 is also the verb to cheat. In a Cantonese dictation, the filler rule now keeps a 呃 right next to another Chinese character, as in 佢呃咗我啲钱. With Clean Up Text on, the local model may still remove it.
