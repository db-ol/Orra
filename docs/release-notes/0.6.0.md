Projects comes as a preview. It sorts the mail of the Mail mailboxes you choose into projects, explains each email with the files of its project and drafts your replies, all on your Mac with the local model. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, Projects, and it may still change.

- Add your projects with their folders and people, and Projects sorts each new email into its project. An email that fits no project waits in Needs a Project. Suggested Projects proposes projects from that mail, and a click on one opens New Project filled in for you.
- Click Explain, and the local model lists what an email says, with sources in the project's files, the email's attachments and earlier mail, and the questions only you can decide. Explanations are in Chinese when Orra's language is Chinese, and in English for every other language, Japanese and Spanish included.
- When an email needs a reply, a draft follows in the email's language, Chinese or English. An email in another language gets no draft. Edit the draft yourself, or type what to change in the box under it and click Change Draft. With the cursor in that box, you can say it with the talk key instead, and the draft changes when you finish speaking. Open Reply in Mail opens Mail's reply window and puts the draft on the clipboard. Press ⌘V there, check the reply and send it yourself.
- Projects reads mail only through the Mail app and only while Mail is open, and works on it only with the local model. It never sends, moves or deletes mail and never connects to a mail server. What it keeps stays on this Mac, and when you turn Projects off, you choose whether to keep it or delete it. Privacy details, in Settings, Projects, lists what it reads and keeps.
- Projects needs a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, the Mail app and the cleanup model of Clean Up Text. If this Mac does not have the model yet, Orra asks before it downloads it (3.05 GB). When macOS asks, allow Orra to control Mail. Then choose the mailboxes and click Start Reading Mail. No mail is read before that. Start Reading Mail opens the Projects window, and later you can open it from Orra's menu.
- Dictation goes first. While you dictate or Live Captions run, Projects pauses its work on the model.
