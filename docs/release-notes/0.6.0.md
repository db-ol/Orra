Projects comes as a preview. It sorts the mail of the Mail mailboxes you choose into projects, explains each email with the files of its project and drafts your replies, all on your Mac with the local model. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, Projects, and it may still change. Live Captions now say what they hear and when they wait.

- Add your projects with their folders and people, and Projects sorts each new email into its project. An email that fits no project waits in Needs a Project. Newsletters and other mail sent to a list or by a machine go to Set Aside, unless a project's people, domains, rules, linked mailbox or threads tie them to it. Suggested Projects proposes projects from the mail in Needs a Project, and a click on one opens New Project filled in for you.
- Click Explain, and the local model lists what an email says, with sources in the project's files, the email's attachments and earlier mail, and the questions only you can decide. Explanations are in Chinese when Orra's language is Chinese, and in English for every other language, Japanese and Spanish included.
- When an email needs a reply, a draft follows in the email's language, Chinese or English. An email that Projects finds to be in another language, such as Japanese, gets no draft. Edit the draft yourself, or type what to change in the box under it and click Change Draft. With the cursor in that box, you can say it with the talk key instead, and the draft changes when you finish speaking. Open Reply in Mail opens Mail's reply window and puts the draft on the clipboard. Press ⌘V there, check the reply and send it yourself.
- Projects reads mail only through the Mail app and only while Mail is open, and works on it only with the local model. It never sends, moves or deletes mail and never connects to a mail server. Assigning, editing and deleting in Projects change only what it keeps on this Mac, never your mail in Mail. That data never leaves this Mac, and when you turn Projects off, you choose whether to keep it or delete it. Privacy details, in Settings, Projects, opens the privacy page, which lists what Projects reads and keeps.
- Projects needs a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, the Mail app and the cleanup model of Clean Up Text. If this Mac does not have the model yet, Orra asks before it downloads it (3.05 GB). When macOS asks, allow Orra to control Mail. Then choose the mailboxes and click Start Reading Mail. No mail is read before that. Start Reading Mail opens the Projects window, and later you can open it from Orra's menu.
- The Projects window opens with How to Use Projects, the steps to make a project, explain an email and send the reply yourself in Mail. After Explain, the explanation and the draft come first, and the email itself follows under Original Email. Hide Explanation shows the email first, with the draft under it, and Show Explanation brings the explanation back.
- Dictation goes first. While you dictate or Live Captions run, Projects pauses its work on the model. The model sorts mail only while the Projects window is open. With the window closed, new mail is sorted by the rules only, and the model sorts the rest once you open the window again.
- With Live Captions on and no caption yet, the caption window now says that captions show what the Mac plays, such as a call or a video, and not what you say into your microphone. While you dictate, it says that captions wait and catch up when you finish. The note about no sound from the Mac now also shows when no audio arrives at all, such as while nothing plays. It says to allow Orra under Screen & System Audio Recording if something is playing.
