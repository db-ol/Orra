Projects, still a preview, is easier to set up and reads more of your files. It searches across Chinese and English, lets you choose the files an explanation uses, and shows an email beside its explanation with Compare with Original. You can also attach the files an email asks for to your reply.

- When you turn on Projects in Settings, Projects, the Set Up Projects checklist under the switch shows the steps from the cleanup model download to your first project, each with a check mark once it is done. Open Mail opens the Mail app where a step or the Projects window needs it, and Orra's menu shows Set Up Projects… while Projects is off.
- Projects now reads Excel and PowerPoint files, in project folders and as attachments, and CSV files in project folders. It knows every other file in a project folder by its name, such as a drawing or a video. In project folders it also recognizes the text of images and scanned PDF pages with the text recognition of macOS, after the other files and between dictations. Text recognition reads Chinese and English, the Latin letters of other languages without their accents, and Japanese only in part. The project's page shows how many images and scans wait, and an excerpt recognized from an image says that its words and numbers may be wrong.
- Explain first has the cleanup model turn the email into search terms in Chinese and English, with other words for the same things, so an email in Chinese finds files in English more often, and the other way round.
- An email in Needs a Project, or one whose search terms match another project's files better than its own, may also get passages of your other projects' folders, labeled with their project. The model writes the draft without them in a second request, which takes a little longer, and a line under the points says so. Forget Project… now also removes the explanations and drafts of other emails that used the project's files.
- Project Files for This Explanation, under What it says, lists the project files the model got, with their folder, whether a point cites them and why they were included, such as Found by search. Its chips show the saved excerpts, by page, slide or sheet.
- Choose Files for This Email…, under that list or in the email's context menu, lets you tick up to 6 files of any project, also before the first Explain. The next explanation puts their best passages first, fills the rest with its own search, and may use them in the draft too. A file outside your project folders is never read. Drag one onto the list of files you can tick, and Orra only offers to add its folder to the project.
- When an email asks you to send a document, Files This Email Asks For quotes the request and lists files that may be it, the email's project first. Tick Attach to Reply, or add files of your project folders with Add File… under Attachments for the Reply, and Open Reply in Mail or Reply All adds them to Mail's reply window. Nothing is ticked until you tick it, and a sender who may not be who they claim gets no files.
- Compare with Original, next to Explain Again, shows the email beside its explanation and draft, each scrolling on its own. The sidebar hides and the mail list stays, and Exit Comparison brings the sidebar back.
- A point that cites the email as [E1] now shows the This email chip, not [E1] with No source provided. Source ids such as [F1] are gone from the questions and the draft, so they no longer go to Mail with your reply. A long line above or under the mail list no longer cuts off the top and bottom of the Projects window.
- Projects still reads only the mailboxes you chose and the files in the folders you added. Text recognition and the new search run on this Mac, with macOS and the cleanup model you already have, so nothing new is downloaded. Orra opens the Mail app only when you click Open Mail.
- Orra attaches only the files you ticked, when you click Open Reply in Mail or Reply All. Mail may save that reply with its attachments to your account's Drafts on the mail server before you send it. If macOS asks again whether Orra may control Mail, its prompt now also says that Orra opens a reply with the files you tick only when you click.
- Once 0.6.1 has opened the Projects data, an earlier version of Orra cannot open it, and says so until you choose Delete All Projects Data… in Settings, Projects.
