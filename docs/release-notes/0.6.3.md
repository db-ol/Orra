This update makes Projects file mail more carefully and keep reading when Mail is slow, and Live Captions now say when nothing is playing.

- Projects files an email into a project only when something points to that project, such as its people, its words or a mailbox linked to it. Mail that nothing points to waits in Needs a Project, also when you have only one project. Emails that an earlier version filed on the cleanup model's choice alone are sorted again once, with the replies filed with them. Emails you moved yourself, or that have a draft, stay where they are.
- Needs reply now marks only mail that asks you something. Mail that has you only in Cc, mail to a group address or sent to you as Bcc no longer shows Needs reply, unless it names you. Neither do forwarded notices whose note asks nothing, or automated mail from addresses such as it-noreply@.
- When Mail is slow to answer for one account, mailbox or email, Projects goes on with the rest and asks for the slow part again later, instead of starting over. New mail shows sooner, ten emails at a time. This helps most with a work or school Exchange account that loses its connection outside the organization's network.
- Live Captions now say when nothing is playing on the Mac, and that captions show what the Mac plays, such as a call or a video, not what you say into your microphone. When something plays but no sound reaches Orra, the window still suggests the permission.
