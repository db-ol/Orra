This update makes cleanup remove words said twice by accident, keeps Orra from learning corrections that only change a number, and joins capitals spelled one by one in Chinese text.

- With Clean up with a local model on, cleanup now also removes words said twice by accident in Chinese and English, such as 我我觉得 or “the the”, as Polish already does. Before, a dictation without fillers or a spoken correction kept them. Repeats you mean stay, such as 看看, 对对对 or “very very”.
- The model can still drop one copy of a word you repeated on purpose, as when 非常非常 becomes 非常, and a character said twice inside a word, as in 中中英, may stay. Dictations with a word said twice, doubled words such as 看看 included, now go to the model too, so cleanup adds some time to them, as it does to dictations with fillers.
- With Learn from my corrections on, a correction that only changes a number, such as Qwen2 to Qwen3 or 十四 to 四十, could be learned as a misheard word, and changing 四点 to 十点 asked to add a word to your vocabulary. Now Orra never learns such a correction and never asks about one, and what an earlier version learned this way no longer changes the numbers in your dictations. A correction that only writes the same number another way, such as Windows十一 to Windows11, is still learned.
- In Chinese text, Orra now joins capitals that the speech model wrote one by one, so P R becomes PR, 3.5G B becomes 3.5GB and O.K. becomes OK. It does so in dictations and in Transcribe File, with no switch of its own. In a dictation that talks about spelling, as in Lee 的拼写是 L E E, the letters stay apart.
