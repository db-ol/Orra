This update lets Orra show what it hears while you speak.

- Show text while you speak is new in Settings, Dictation, right under Dictation language, and off until you turn it on. With it on, Orra shows what it hears above the bars at the bottom of the screen while you speak, without taking the focus from the app you are in. Orra still pastes once, when the dictation ends, and the pasted text can differ from the text shown. The text shown is never pasted, saved or logged. The speech model works while you speak, which uses more power.
