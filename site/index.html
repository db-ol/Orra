Orra

Speak,
and it’s written.

Dictate and translate in any app. Everything runs on your Mac.

Download for Mac

Version 0.3.2 Needs a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15.6 or later and about 3 GB of free space.

Made for the way
you actually talk.

Your vocabulary

Add the names and terms you use, and Orra writes them your way.

Learns from your corrections

Correct a misheard word after a dictation, and Orra remembers what it wrote and how you fixed it. Next time it writes the word your way. Off by default. It never keeps whole sentences.

Chinese and English, mixed

Speak Chinese, English, or both in one sentence.

Translation

Speak Chinese and get English, or speak English and get Chinese. It runs on your Mac.

Privacy

What you say stays on your Mac.

You need the internet only to download Orra and its models. Apart from that, Orra goes online only to check for updates, and only if you allow it. Everything else runs on your Mac, and Orra never saves your recordings or what you said. The README has the details.

Open source

Orra is written in Swift and SwiftUI and released under the MIT License. Read the code, build it yourself, or help make it better.
