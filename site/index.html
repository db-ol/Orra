Orra

Hold a key.
Speak. Let go.

Dictate anywhere on your Mac. Speech becomes text on your Mac, never on a server.

Download for Mac

Version 0.3.0. Needs macOS 15.6 or later on Apple silicon.

Three steps.
Any app you can type in.

  1. 1

    Hold a key

    Hold the talk key, right Control unless you pick another. Or press it twice quickly to talk hands free.

  2. 2

    Speak

    Orra records while you hold it. White bars in a small capsule at the bottom of the screen move with your voice.

  3. 3

    Release

    Orra turns your speech into text on your Mac and pastes it where your cursor is, in any app you can type in.

The first time, a welcome window lets you pick its language and walks you through downloading the speech model (2.47 GB, once), allowing the microphone and Accessibility, and choosing your talk key.

Made for the way
you actually talk.

Chinese, English and both at once

Speak Chinese, English, or mix them in one sentence.

On device speech recognition

Qwen3-ASR 1.7B runs on your Mac. What you say does not leave it.

Your talk key, or hands free

Choose fn (Globe), or the left or right Control, Option or Command key, by pressing it. For a longer text, press it twice quickly and talk hands free.

Clean text

Fillers such as um, uh and 呃 are removed, and numbers spoken in Chinese, such as dates, times, prices and percentages, are written as digits.

Your vocabulary

Add up to 5,000 names and terms, and Orra writes them your way.

Cleanup on your Mac

Turn on Clean Up Text and a local model removes the fillers left and applies spoken corrections such as "Monday, I mean Tuesday". It uses a 3.05 GB model you download once, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Translation between Chinese and English

Hold the translation key, right Option unless you pick another (right Command when right Option is your talk key), and speak. Orra pastes what you said translated, Chinese into English and English into Chinese, or always into English or always into Chinese. It runs on your Mac with the same downloaded model as cleanup, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Learns from your corrections

Turn it on, fix a misheard word after a dictation in apps such as Notes, Mail and Safari, and Orra adds the right spelling to your vocabulary. A notice lets you undo it.

Updates in the app

Check for Updates in the menu, or let Orra check for you. Nothing installs until you choose it.

English and Chinese interface

Menus and windows are in English or Simplified Chinese. They follow your Mac's language, or the one you choose in Settings.

Privacy

What you say stays on your Mac.

Orra turns your speech into text on your Mac and never sends your recordings or their text anywhere. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own.

Orra goes online for three things only:

  • Downloading the speech model, after you choose Download Speech Model. It comes from Hugging Face, or from ModelScope or hf-mirror.com when Hugging Face cannot be reached. Orra checks the size and SHA-256 hash of every file before using it.
  • Downloading the cleanup model, only after you turn on Clean Up Text or Translation and agree to its download. It comes from the same servers and is checked the same way.
  • Checking for updates, only after you allow it. Launching Orra never touches the network before that.

Learning from your corrections is off until you turn it on. Even then Orra keeps only word pairs, never your text. The README has the full details.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers.
