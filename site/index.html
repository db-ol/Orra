Orra

Speak,
and it’s written.

Dictate and translate in any app, and in your calls. Everything runs on your Mac.

Download for Mac

Version 0.5.0 Needs a Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15.6 or later and about 6 GB of free space.

Made for the way
you actually talk.

Your vocabulary

Add the names and terms you use, and Orra writes them your way.

Learns from your corrections

Correct a misheard word after a dictation, and Orra remembers what it wrote and how you fixed it. Next time it writes the word your way. Off by default. It never keeps whole sentences.

Two languages, mixed

Speak Chinese, English, or both in one sentence. Under Dictation language in Settings you can pick Japanese or Spanish with English instead, as a beta.

Translation

Press the translation key and speak, and Orra translates between English and your dictation language, Chinese, Japanese or Spanish. It runs on your Mac.

Meeting Interpretation

Read what others say in your language, and let them hear you in theirs, even in your own voice.

Live Captions

Orra shows what your Mac plays, such as a call, as translated captions in a floating window. It translates with the same model on your Mac as the translation key. It never uses the microphone, and captions stay in memory only.

Microphone Orra Microphone

Speak Translations to Others

With Translation and Speak Translations to Others on in Settings, press the translation key and speak. A voice model on your Mac speaks the translation into Orra Microphone. Choose Orra Microphone as the microphone in your call app, and the other side hears your words in their language. Record a short voice sample, and it speaks in your own voice. Use only your own voice, and tell others they hear a synthesized voice.

The voice model is a download of about 2 GB. Orra Microphone is a separate download.

Privacy

What you say stays on your Mac.

You need the internet only to download Orra and its models. Apart from that, Orra goes online only to check for updates, and only if you allow it. Everything else runs on your Mac, and Orra never saves your recordings or what you said. The one exception is the audio of your voice sample, if you turn on Remember my voice. Orra never saves its words. The privacy details are on GitHub.
